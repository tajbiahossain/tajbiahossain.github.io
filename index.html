Researchinterests

Robust, data-efficient representations for messy text

My undergraduate research at AUST, supervised by Raqeebir Rab, tackled text classification on heavily imbalanced data. We combined SVD and GloVe embeddings with ensemble and deep learning models to recover performance on minority classes that standard training tends to ignore.

For graduate study I want to push this further: algorithmic frameworks that stay reliable under skewed, scarce and noisy data, and that adapt and deploy efficiently.
